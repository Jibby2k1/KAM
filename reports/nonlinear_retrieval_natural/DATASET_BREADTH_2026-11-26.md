# Dataset breadth, temporal memory, and controlled noise

Research handoff · November 26, 2026

The fresh HotpotQA study established that a question-conditioned pair scorer can distinguish many supporting links from incidental links. This follow-up tested where that result transfers, which datasets make memory failures measurable, and which model and system changes carry across controlled shifts. The study includes Wikipedia, news, scientific papers, historical prose, conversation histories, an actual revision archive, and generated record histories.

The earlier [fresh HotpotQA study](SUPPORT_LINKS.md) used 768 held-out questions. The context head recovered 323/368 linked support pairs and 23/100 unlinked pairs, compared with 108/368 and 28/100 for sequential. Both methods had zero complete evidence on the 300 questions missing at least one support in the shared candidate pool. The headline result for ranking an actual supporting link among linked alternatives was 316/360 versus 209/360 for the original pair head. The linked-only auxiliary objective performed similarly to the ordinary objective with the same features.

## Dataset choices

- **Real revisions.** Use the Philadelphia Fed worksheets first for exact selection and unavailability, with query-to-series identity evaluated independently. Preserve the known answer when adding wrong-series records, neighboring observations and later vintages. Evaluate identity shift with reserved formulations and a held-out series.
- **Natural temporal evidence.** Use TimeQA to distinguish event time from other dates in prose. Use TempReason for parseable entity and interval controls. LongMemEval-S provides a conversational track with explicit session evidence. Report the author’s full context and the strictly eligible as-of subset separately because 44 questions have some labeled evidence dated after their question.
- **Cheap controlled noise.** Procedural histories can independently vary query date, observation date, arrival, ambiguity, revision order and history size. Serial UnSeenTimeQA yielded 3,600 executable labels whose values and evidence-event IDs survived independent time shifts. TimelineQA generated over a million episodes and provided a matched test of false date provenance. NoLiMa’s direct and latent needles test lexical distraction with exact passage IDs.
- **Domain transfer.** Retain 2Wiki and MuSiQue as complementary tests. The news and scientific-paper pilots diagnose poor evidence visibility and low reader accuracy, respectively. Use them to establish chunk and reader ceilings before asking pair-selection questions.

Small fits and evaluation shards ran in parallel, using L4 and RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell GPUs. The independently implemented archive replay ran on CPUs. One failed identity run used 13 GPU-seconds and succeeded on retry. One pending run was superseded before allocating a GPU. These totals cover the follow-up studies in this report. ## Interpretation

The natural QA studies use frozen pretrained text encoders and a separate reader, with KAM-inspired retrieval heads. The architectural claims come from the direct KAM decoder experiments with typed records. Exact masks enforce dates and identity only when their inputs are trusted. The metadata experiments measure sensitivity to controlled edits and eligibility constraints. GPU timings distinguish routing or sorting with resident inputs from end-to-end serving. The reports include sample restrictions, comparison budgets, reader ceilings, paired uncertainty where applicable, and exact result counts.
