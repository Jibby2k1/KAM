# Dataset portfolio for retrieval and temporal memory

Use three complementary evaluation regimes. Frozen natural evidence tests measure transfer. Historical snapshots test as-of correctness with actual revisions. Procedural histories isolate failure mechanisms with exact executable labels. Each regime answers a different question about KAM and the associated retrieval methods.

| Dataset | Evaluation unit | Evidence and exact supervision | Controlled noise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [2WikiMultiHopQA](https://aclanthology.org/2020.coling-main.580/) | Question with supplied Wikipedia paragraphs | Supporting titles and facts, plus Wikidata evidence paths | Add an irrelevant cross-reference to a non-supporting paragraph. Match the syntax and token count with a sham reference. |
| [MuSiQue](https://aclanthology.org/2022.tacl-1.31/) | Question with two, three, or four supporting paragraphs | Paragraph IDs, answer aliases, and decomposition annotations | Perturb one distractor or use the authors’ unanswerable contrasts. Report evidence cardinality separately. |
| [IIRC](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.86/) | Question anchored in a known paragraph with real outgoing hyperlinks | Answerer-marked supporting spans and explicit unanswerable labels | Retain all archived hyperlinks as natural distractors and distinguish missing support in the selected articles from underlying unanswerability. [Frozen transfer result](../../reports/nonlinear_retrieval_natural/IIRC_PAIR_TRANSFER.md) |
| [MultiHop-RAG](https://github.com/yixuantt/MultiHop-RAG) | Question over an independent news corpus | Evidence articles and quoted fact spans | Add independent articles or wrong-date passages. Score article and span visibility separately. |
| [QASPER](https://aclanthology.org/2021.naacl-main.365/) | Question over a scientific paper | Alternative annotator evidence sets and answer annotations | Append paragraphs from other papers drawn from similar sections. Hold each paper together and report unresolved figure or table evidence. |
| [TimeQA](https://github.com/wenhuchen/Time-Sensitive-QA) | Entity and relation queried across two periods | Crowd-verified answer spans, paragraph locations, and date windows | Add an opposite-period passage with a matching review date. Use an otherwise identical passage with an unrelated review date as the sham. |
| [TempReason](https://github.com/DAMO-NLP-SG/TempReason) | Absolute-date or relative-event question | Explicit fact intervals and accepted answer strings | Add facts for other entities with the same relation. Keep entity filtering and temporal filtering as separate controls. |
| [UnSeenTimeQA](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.94/) | Fictional logistics schedule and static or relative time | Executable serial event trace and exact set-valued location labels | Shift the time of day for the entire schedule, including the question. | [LongMemEval-S](https://github.com/xiaowu0162/LongMemEval) | Chat history with dated sessions | Annotated answer-session IDs and timestamp audit | Add the same lexical decoy before and after the cutoff and check exact session ranks. | [TimelineQA](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-acl.6/) | Generated lifelog at sparse, medium or dense event rates | Event and persona IDs, authoritative days, and atomic QA pairs | Add episodes from other days or assign a misleading date to a same-template episode. Preserve the ground truth in the structured timeline. |
| [Philadelphia Fed real-time macroeconomic archives](https://www.philadelphiafed.org/surveys-and-data/real-time-data-research/real-time-data-set-for-macroeconomists) | Series, observed quarter, and vintage cutoff | An exact historical workbook cell, or a confirmed missing cell | Add later publications, superseded values, neighboring observations, and similar series. Simulate a separate arrival clock to test delayed ingestion. |
| [Procedural revision histories](../../scripts/run_kam_bitemporal_noise.py) | Alias binding followed by a versioned fact lookup | An independent lexicographic oracle for address, revision, and payload | Generate arbitrarily many known distractors, vary the history length, and change exactly one record for counterfactual checks. |

## Recommended uses

1. **Use the vintage archive for real revision memory.** Publication and observation coordinates are independently recorded. Out-of-window and missing-at-cutoff answers can be audited without a generative judge. Keep the archived numerical representation as the target because later vintages can include rebasing. Delay the local ingestion clock only in an explicitly simulated study.
2. **Use TimeQA for natural prose and TempReason for structured timing controls.** TimeQA asks whether the reader and selector can identify the applicable passage when the same entity has different answers across time. TempReason gives parsable interval and event-order references, plus naturally matching distractor relation types. Hold out entities and report where explicit boundary semantics are ambiguous.
3. **Use procedural histories for scaling and architecture.** Vary the number of candidates, nuisance records, observation order, and clock origin independently. Preserve the gold lookup by construction and compare against an independent oracle. The task can expose positional shortcuts and temporal admission errors before the same method is tried on unstructured text.
4. **Use 2Wiki and MuSiQue together for pair-selection claims.** 2Wiki supports clear link and noise diagnostics. MuSiQue tests connected evidence and varying support cardinality. Retain an explicit sequential control and an oracle reader. Article title mentions are a useful feature whose reliability varies with the source and with preprocessing.
5. **Require a usable reader floor for news and scholarly prose.** Both domains broaden the evidence base. Chunk coverage, format suitability, and gold-evidence reader performance must be reported before a reader score can support a selector comparison.

## Additional datasets to screen

- **TimelineQA aggregation** extends the atomic probe to exact monthly counts. The fixture independently verifies event IDs in generated tables and measures false-date interference at matched evidence budgets. [Executable count study](../../reports/nonlinear_retrieval_natural/TIMELINEQA_COUNTS.md) · [Author benchmark repository](https://github.com/facebookresearch/TimelineQA)
- **UnSeenTimeQA hard and hypothetical** provide a next test of dependency-aware rescheduling. The exact audit covers only serial static and relative questions with explicit event clocks. Check the hard, parallel, and hypothetical conventions independently before generating scored counterfactuals. [Author paper](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.94/)
- **NoLiMa** reduces lexical overlap between a needle and its query. It is a direct challenge for raw query-term coverage, which is useful on some sources and brittle under lexical distractions. [Author paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/modarressi25a.html)
- **RULER** offers seeded long-context tasks with explicit parameters for evidence count, distance, and filler. Use it to independently audit position and depth effects. Preserve the generator revision, tokenizer, and actual token lengths. [Author benchmark repository](https://github.com/NVIDIA/RULER)

## Evaluation contract

Freeze query IDs, title or entity reservations, source digests, and benchmark-specific normalization before the first scored run. Use the same candidate pool and evidence budget for paired comparisons. Keep candidate coverage, support selection, reader accuracy, and joint correctness separately inspectable. Report unknown-identity abstention separately from resolution among supported identities. For synthetic noise, retain clean and sham controls, document which data fields were changed, and independently verify the answer remains valid.
